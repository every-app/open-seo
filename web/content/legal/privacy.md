## I. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect personal information and information that has been aggregated or de-identified. Personal information may include your name, email address, account credentials or authentication information, organization and billing identifiers, communications, device and network identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, and information you submit to or generate through the Site or Service. Usage information, including IP addresses, may be personal information even when it does not identify a person by name.

### 1. _Information collected via Technology_

To receive marketing communications from us, you only need to submit your email address. To use the hosted version of the Service thereafter, you may need to submit further Personal Information, such as your name, email address, password, organization information, billing or customer identifiers, and other information you choose to provide through the Service. However, in an effort to improve the quality of the Service, we track information provided to us by your browser or by our software application when you view or use the Service, such as the website you came from (known as the “referring URL”), the pages you visit, the type of browser you use, the device from which you connected to the Service, your operating system, approximate location derived from IP address, the time and date of access, and other information related to how you use the Site or Service. In the authenticated hosted Service, PostHog may also collect session replay recordings to help us diagnose bugs and improve usability; form inputs and designated sensitive text are masked before recording.

We may collect site addresses, keywords, organization & project data, reports, prompts, search and audit queries, outputs, and information retrieved from authorized integrations or SEO data providers when you use those features. Some of this information may relate to other individuals, including your clients’ personnel or website visitors. We also collect messages and attachments you provide to support. The categories available to us depend on the features you use and the data you choose to submit.

**Advertising and referral measurement.** We may deploy pixels, cookies, SDKs, tags or server-side event connections to measure ad views, clicks, referrals, sign-ups and purchases, attribute conversions, and, where enabled, deliver or measure targeted advertising. When deployed, these tools may disclose device and online identifiers, IP addresses, page and event information, referral identifiers, and relevant transaction events to advertising or referral partners. Advertising partners may use the information under their own terms, including for cross-context advertising where applicable. Planned partners may include Meta or Reddit; naming them here does not mean their tools are currently enabled. Other advertising platforms may be added subject to the disclosures and choices described here. We identify the partners actually deployed and their purposes in the notice or preference interface provided with the tools; a reference to a category of future partners does not authorize an undisclosed new use. Advertising/conversion choices are distinct from product analytics and session replay. We obtain consent before collection where required and honor applicable sale/sharing or targeted-advertising opt-outs, including qualifying preference signals. Google API user data, support content, and Organization & Project content are not used for advertising or sent to advertising pixels.

We use technologies necessary for authentication and security. We may use a cookieless public-site analytics provider, including Plausible Analytics, to measure website visits and use. We may also use referral-attribution technologies and, within the hosted service, PostHog analytics and session replay. These technologies have different data practices and may require different notices or choices. We describe the providers actually in use and the choices actually available in the current notice presented with the relevant feature.

## III. GOOGLE USER DATA

You may choose to connect Google services, such as Google Search Console and Google Analytics, to the hosted Service, or to sign in with your Google account. When you do, the Service accesses Google user data through Google's APIs with your authorization. This section describes how we handle that data, in addition to the other terms of this Privacy Policy. Self-hosted deployments of OpenSEO connect to Google using the operator's own credentials, and this section does not apply to them.

**What Google user data we access.** If you sign in with Google, we receive your basic account information (name, email address, and profile picture) to create and authenticate your account. If you connect Google Search Console, we access search performance data for the sites you authorize, such as queries, pages, clicks, impressions, and average positions, as well as URL inspection results. If you connect Google Analytics, we access report data for the property you select, such as traffic and acquisition metrics, landing page and page performance, key events, ecommerce outcomes, site search terms, and audience breakdowns by device and country, along with property configuration details such as your list of properties, data streams, and measurement settings. We also receive the email address of the connected Google account so we can show you which account is linked. All Google API scopes we request for these integrations are read-only; we cannot modify data in your Google account.

**How we use it.** We use Google user data solely to provide the user-facing features of the Service that you request: showing connection status, generating SEO reports and insights, and returning data you ask for through the Service, including through AI assistants and MCP clients that you explicitly connect. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not use it to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine learning models. Support personnel will not submit Google user data to support AI tools except where separately authorized and permitted under applicable Google requirements.

**How we share it.** We do not sell Google user data, and we do not transfer it to data brokers or advertisers. Google user data leaves our systems only in these cases below and the limited support processing expressly described in this section: (a) when you connect the Service to an AI assistant or other MCP client and request data through it, the results are returned to that client, whose own privacy terms then apply; (b) when you use the in-app AI assistant, relevant data may be processed by an AI model accessed through providers such as OpenRouter or OpenAI in order to generate your response; and (c) our infrastructure provider, Cloudflare, hosts the systems that process this data on our behalf. We transfer Google user data only as expressly described in this section, including (a) back to an AI assistant or MCP client that you connect and ask to retrieve it, (b) to OpenRouter or OpenAI, under a no-training setting, solely to generate the response you requested in the Service, and (c) to Cloudflare to host that processing. OpenAI or Anthropic support agents may receive Google API user data only where that specific support use and transfer is permitted by the Google API Services User Data Policy and any required user authorization has been obtained; they do not receive blanket access to connected accounts. Transfers remain limited to the permitted user-facing or user-directed purpose and no-training settings. We do not use Google user data to train generalized models, for advertising, or for human review except to comply with law or with your specific request. Google report data may be retained in in-app agent chat sessions or other Organization & Project data even where the retrieval flow does not write report payloads to our databases. Retained reports, prompts, chat histories and generated outputs are subject to the deletion periods below. New historical-data features will be disclosed before activation, with any consent required for a changed Google-data use.

**How we protect it.** The OAuth tokens that grant access to your Google account are stored encrypted at rest, and all data is transferred over encrypted connections (TLS). We retrieve authorized Google report data to provide the features you request. Report data may also be included in and retained with in-app agent chat sessions. Related reports, prompts, and generated outputs may be retained where the hosted service saves them. We do not use Google user data for advertising or to train generalized AI models. OpenRouter and OpenAI process relevant in-product prompts using the no-training settings we have enabled; other transfers occur only as described here and subject to applicable Google API requirements.

**Retention and deletion.** Google report data retained in chat histories or Organization & Project data is kept until deletion or account closure, then removed from active systems within 30 days, subject to lawful retention exceptions and backup overwriting within 35 days. Disconnecting an integration does not by itself delete previously retained chat or report content. While an integration is connected, we retain the OAuth tokens and connection details (such as the selected site or property and the connected account email). Disconnecting an integration in your settings removes that connection and its details immediately, and the stored tokens for the Google account are deleted once none of your projects use it. You can also revoke OpenSEO's access at any time from your Google account's security settings at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting your OpenSEO account disables its Google connections and initiates deletion of associated data from active systems, subject to lawful retention obligations and backup processes described below.

**Limited Use.** OpenSEO's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
