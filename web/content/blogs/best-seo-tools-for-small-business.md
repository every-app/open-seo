## How to choose

Start with OpenSEO. It does keyword research, rank tracking, backlinks, site audits, local SEO, and AI search tracking, for one site or many client sites. Then look at one of the others only if you need what it does best:

- **Backlinks are how you grow:** Ahrefs has the biggest backlink index.
- **You also research competitors' ads and content:** Semrush puts SEO, ads, and content tools in one place.
- **You track thousands of keywords every day:** OpenSEO charges per check, and 100 keywords checked daily is up to $45 a month. At thousands of keywords a day, SE Ranking's flat $129 plan costs less.

Whichever you pick, set up Google Search Console first. It's free, and it's the only tool that shows what Google actually sends you.

## Google Search Console: free and essential

[Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) is Google's own free tool. It shows the searches your site appears for, how many people click, your average position, and pages Google can't index. No paid tool has this data, because it comes straight from Google.

It only shows your own site, though. You can't research competitors or find keywords you don't rank for yet. That's what OpenSEO adds, and you can connect Search Console to OpenSEO to see both in one place.

If you're a local business, also claim your free [Google Business Profile](https://www.google.com/business/). It decides how you show up in Google Maps.

## Ahrefs: the biggest backlink index

Ahrefs runs its own crawler, has recorded 35 trillion backlinks, and updates its index every 15 to 30 minutes. If link building is how you grow, no other tool here matches its backlink data.

- **What it costs:** Lite is $129 a month for one user, with 1,000 credits a month. Starter is $29 a month with 200 credits and no MCP. There's no free trial. See our [Ahrefs pricing breakdown](/ahrefs-pricing).
- **The catch:** opening a report uses a credit, and so does each filter you apply, so exploring runs credits down fast. See [Ahrefs alternatives](/ahrefs-alternative).

## Semrush: SEO, ads, and content in one suite

Semrush is the biggest all-in-one marketing suite. Next to keyword research, rank tracking, and site audits, it sells toolkits for competitors' ads, content writing, local listings, and traffic and market data.

- **What it costs:** the SEO plan is $139 a month, or $1,408 for a year paid upfront (about $117 a month), for 5 websites and 500 tracked keywords. AI visibility tracking and MCP access start with the Starter plan at $199. Full details are in our [Semrush pricing breakdown](/semrush-pricing).
- **The catch:** the toolkits are extra. Local is $30 or $60 a month per location, Advertising is $99 or more, and each extra user is $45 a month or more. See [Semrush alternatives](/semrush-alternative).

## SE Ranking: the most tracked keywords for the price

SE Ranking covers keyword research, competitor analysis, site audits, and rank tracking, with the highest tracking limits of the three paid suites here.

- **What it costs:** Core is $129 a month, or $1,238 for a year paid upfront (about $103 a month), with 10 projects and 2,000 keywords tracked daily. Growth is $279 a month for 30 projects and 5,000 keywords. There's a 14-day free trial.
- **The catch:** Core is the cheapest plan, so a business with one site and a few dozen keywords pays for limits it won't use.

## How much should a small business spend on SEO tools?

Start at $0 with Google Search Console. Once you're writing pages to rank or keeping an eye on competitors, $10 to $50 a month covers most small businesses. A tool over $100 a month earns its place only if someone uses it every week. If nobody logs in, cancel it.

## FAQ

### What's the best SEO tool for a small business?

OpenSEO, paired with the free Google Search Console. OpenSEO covers keyword research, rank tracking, backlinks, site audits, and local SEO from $10 a month. ### Is there a free SEO tool for small businesses?

Yes, for your own site. Google Search Console and Google Business Profile are free. No tool researches competitors or new keywords for free, because that data costs money to collect. OpenSEO gives you $0.50 of trial credits without a card, then costs $10 a month.

### Is Semrush worth it for a small business?

If you'll use its ads, content, and traffic toolkits, yes. For SEO alone, the SEO plan is $139 a month for jobs OpenSEO does from $10 a month.

### Is Ahrefs worth it for a small business?

If link building is your main way to grow, yes: its backlink index is the biggest. Otherwise OpenSEO shows your backlinks and your competitors' from $10 a month, against $129 for Ahrefs Lite.

### Is OpenSEO good for agencies?

Yes. There are no per-seat fees or project limits, your team shares projects and credits, and you can send clients reports by link or PDF. A freelancer managing 15 client sites spends up to about $41 a month.

### Can I do SEO with ChatGPT or Claude?

Yes, if you connect them to real SEO data. On their own they guess at search volumes and rankings. Connected to OpenSEO through [MCP](/docs/mcp), they can pull real keyword data, check rankings, and run audits, then explain what to do next.

### What's the best SEO tool for a local business?
