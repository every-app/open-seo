## What SEOShark is

SEOShark is an SEO platform for business owners, marketers and agencies. It covers keyword research, competitor and backlink research, rank tracking, site audits and AI-search visibility, all on the same data source and connected to each other.

It is built for you and your AI agent to work together. Through [MCP](/docs/mcp), an agent such as Claude Code, Codex or Cursor can research live data, read what you have already saved about your business, and write its findings back to your project.

## Built for how AI agents actually work

An AI agent needs access to the right tools and data to do useful SEO work. MCP makes that access possible; the design of the tools matters just as much.

Each tool is built around the task someone is trying to accomplish. Can the agent reach the capabilities it needs? Does the response include what it needs to make a decision, or bury it in data that is not relevant?

That thinking also led to [shared project context](/docs/skills/seo-project-setup). You save your business goals, audience, competitors and writing preferences once, and your agent reads them in every later session. Each new research task builds on what you have already told it.

## Turning research into a report you can use

Research should end with a report you can return to and use.

SEOShark's [agent skills](/docs/skills) guide each workflow toward a [saved report](/docs/skills/seo-report). A competitor analysis explains what other sites are doing and recommends opportunities for your business. A site audit organizes the problems it finds and helps you decide what to fix first. The report's content and layout fit the question you are asking.

You can open the report in your project, share it, and ask your agent to read it before starting the next task. That gives the work a home beyond the conversation where it happened.
