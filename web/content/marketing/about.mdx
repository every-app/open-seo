## How OpenSEO started

I started looking into SEO for another project I was working on. I was new to it, and the tools I tried felt overwhelming. I also wanted to work with the AI tools I was already using, but couldn't use them with SEO software the way I wanted.

Then I found [DataForSEO](https://dataforseo.com/), an API that provides much of the data you need for SEO research. I saw people building their own tools on top of it and wondered whether we could build something useful together.

I [asked the TechSEO community on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/TechSEO/comments/1r7qifp/open_source_seo_tool_that_uses_your_own/) whether an open-source tool would be useful. People were interested, and they raised good questions about the data and what the tool would need to do. The following week, I [shared the first version of OpenSEO](https://www.reddit.com/r/TechSEO/comments/1rh9jn6/open_source_semrush_alternative_built_on/).

## Building for how AI agents actually work

An AI agent needs access to the right tools and data to do useful SEO work. [MCP](/docs/mcp), the connection that lets an agent use tools like OpenSEO, makes that access possible. The design of those tools matters just as much.

When I'm building one, I think about the task someone is trying to accomplish. Can the agent access the capabilities it needs? Does the response include the information it needs to make a decision, or bury it in data that isn't relevant?

That thinking also led to [shared project context](/docs/skills/seo-project-setup). You can save your business goals, audience, and writing preferences so your agent can read them in later sessions. Each new research task can build on what you've already told it about your business.

## Turning research into a report you can use

I want you to finish a research task with a report you can return to and use.

OpenSEO's agent skills guide research tasks toward a [saved report](/docs/skills/seo-report). A competitor analysis can explain what other sites are doing and recommend opportunities for your business. A site audit can organize the problems it finds and help you decide what to fix first. The report's content and layout can fit the question you're asking.

You can open the report in your project, share it, and ask your agent to read it before starting the next task. That gives the work a home beyond the conversation where it happened.

## Why I believe in open source

I believe open source is the future of software. People should be able to inspect and change the tools their businesses depend on.

With OpenSEO, you can read the code, adapt it to your workflow, contribute improvements, or self-host it. You can also use our hosted service. I want people to choose that service because it's useful, convenient, and fairly priced.

That choice matters to how we build the company. We have to keep earning your business. You can read more about [why OpenSEO is open source](/open-source-seo) or [explore the code on GitHub](https://github.com/every-app/open-seo).
