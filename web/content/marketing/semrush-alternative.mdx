## Why people look for a Semrush alternative

- **The price.** $139 a month is a lot for one website.
- **The add-ons.** Local, content, ads, social, and traffic data are each sold separately.
- **Per-user pricing.** Every extra person costs at least $45 a month.
- **Canceling takes effort.** You fill in a form, then confirm through an email link within 24 hours, or billing continues. Monthly plans aren't refundable.
- **It's a lot to learn.** Semrush has 55 tools. That's great if marketing is your full-time job, and overwhelming if it isn't.

## Is OpenSEO's data as good as Semrush's?

Semrush builds its own databases, and they're bigger: it reports 28.8 billion keywords and history back to 2012. OpenSEO uses [DataForSEO](https://dataforseo.com/), which sells SEO data to tools and agencies, and covers keyword research in 143 countries.

Either way, search volumes and traffic numbers are estimates, and SEO tools often disagree with each other. For your own site, Google Search Console has the real numbers, and OpenSEO includes them at no extra cost.

## How to switch from Semrush

1. Sign up. You get trial credits, and you don't need a card.
2. Create a project for your site and connect Google Search Console.
3. Export your tracked keywords from Semrush and paste them into an OpenSEO rank tracker.

5. Connect your AI agent to `https://app.openseo.so/mcp` and ask it what to work on first.
6. Cancel Semrush before it renews. Use its [cancellation form](https://www.semrush.com/kb/252-cancelling-your-account), then click the confirmation link Semrush emails you.

## FAQ

### Is there a free Semrush alternative?

Not for keyword and competitor research, since that data costs money in every tool. For your own site, Google Search Console is free, and Semrush's free plan gives you 10 reports a day. OpenSEO is free to try, then $10 a month with $10 of usage included.

### Is OpenSEO cheaper than Semrush?

For most people, yes: $10 a month with $10 of usage included, against $139 for Semrush's cheapest plan. Heavy users pay more, and daily rank tracking adds up fastest. Tracking 500 keywords every day costs about $38 a month.

### Does OpenSEO have an MCP server like Semrush?

Yes. Add `https://app.openseo.so/mcp` to Claude, Codex, Cursor, or another MCP client. It's included from the free trial on. Semrush's MCP starts at $199 a month.

### Is Semrush owned by Adobe?

Yes. Adobe bought Semrush in April 2026.

### What about white-label reports?

Your AI agent can make them. Through MCP, it pulls a client's rankings, keywords, and backlinks, then saves a finished report to their project, which you can share by link or print to PDF with your branding. Save the format as a report template, or turn it into your own skill, and every client gets the same report each month.

### Can my team use OpenSEO?

Yes. Invite teammates by email. There's no per-seat fee.

### Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from the billing portal. If OpenSEO isn't for you, email support@openseo.so within 30 days of a charge for a refund.
