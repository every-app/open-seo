Ahrefs is an SEO suite best known for its backlink data. Its Lite plan is $129 a month for one user, with 1,000 credits a month: opening a report uses one, and so does each filter you apply. There's no free trial.

OpenSEO is an open source SEO platform for business owners and marketers. It's $10 a month, including $10 of usage, and it works with AI agents like Claude through [MCP](/features/mcp).

| You own a business                      | You're an SEO pro or agency        |

## Why people look for an Ahrefs alternative

- **Credits run out.** On Lite, every report, filter, and date change uses a credit. "Every single click costs you," as one Trustpilot reviewer put it.
- **Teams pay per person.** Lite allows two extra users, at up to $40 a month each.
- **Good features cost more.** Content Explorer needs the $249 plan, and the $29 plan has no MCP.
- **No trial, few refunds.** You can't try Ahrefs first, and refunds are only for monthly plans you haven't used.

## What's different about OpenSEO

### You pay for data, not clicks

Ahrefs Lite counts your clicks against a monthly allowance. OpenSEO charges for new data instead, so going back over results and exporting them don't use credits. There's one plan with every feature, unlimited projects, and no per-seat fees.

### It's built for business owners, not just SEO pros

Ahrefs is a professional's tool, with dozens of reports and a filter for everything. OpenSEO focuses on the SEO work most businesses need. If you're new to SEO, the [SEO coach skill](/docs/skills/seo-coach) explains what the numbers mean and what to do next.

## Is OpenSEO's backlink data as good as Ahrefs'?

Backlinks are Ahrefs' strength. It runs its own crawler, has recorded 35 trillion backlinks, and updates its index every 15 to 30 minutes. OpenSEO uses [DataForSEO](https://dataforseo.com/)'s index, which is smaller.

For your own site and your competitors, OpenSEO shows backlinks, referring domains, anchor text, spam scores, and new and lost links over the past year. That's enough to check your links, clean up spam, and see who links to your competitors. If link building is your full-time job, stay with Ahrefs.

## Free options

If you only need data on your own site, start free. Ahrefs Free (formerly Ahrefs Webmaster Tools) audits sites you own and shows up to 1,000 of their backlinks and keywords. Google Search Console shows your real clicks and rankings. Neither lets you research competitors or find new keywords, and that's where a paid tool comes in.

## How to switch from Ahrefs

1. Sign up. You get trial credits, and you don't need a card.
2. Create a project for your site and connect Google Search Console.
3. Export your keywords from Ahrefs Rank Tracker and paste them into an OpenSEO rank tracker.
4. Pull backlinks for your site and your top competitors, then run a site audit.
5. Connect your AI agent to `https://app.openseo.so/mcp`.
6. Cancel Ahrefs under Account Settings, then Billing. Your plan runs to the end of the period, then drops to Ahrefs Free.

## FAQ

### Is there a free Ahrefs alternative?

Not for keyword and competitor research, since that data costs money in every tool. For your own site, Ahrefs Free and Google Search Console are both free. OpenSEO is free to try, then $10 a month with $10 of usage included.

### Is OpenSEO cheaper than Ahrefs?

For most people, yes: $10 a month with $10 of usage included, against $129 for Ahrefs Lite. Tracking the same 750 keywords weekly that Lite includes costs about $8 a month in OpenSEO. Heavy research costs more.

### How are OpenSEO credits different from Ahrefs credits?

Ahrefs credits count clicks: reports, filters, date changes. OpenSEO credits are money, spent when you look up new data. Going back over results you already have is free.

### Does OpenSEO have an MCP server like Ahrefs?

Yes. Add `https://app.openseo.so/mcp` to Claude, Codex, Cursor, or another MCP client. It's included from the free trial on. Ahrefs MCP needs the $129 Lite plan or higher.

### What about white-label reports?

Your AI agent can make them. Through MCP, it pulls a client's rankings, keywords, and backlinks, then saves a finished report to their project, which you can share by link or print to PDF with your branding. Save the format as a report template, or turn it into your own skill, and every client gets the same report each month.

### Can my team use OpenSEO?

Yes. Invite teammates by email. There's no per-seat fee.

### Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from the billing portal. If OpenSEO isn't for you, email support@openseo.so within 30 days of a charge for a refund.
