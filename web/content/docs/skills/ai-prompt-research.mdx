Ask your assistant what people ask ChatGPT about your market. It researches real prompts, groups them by what the person wants, and shows where your brand already appears and which sites get cited instead.

Prompt research does not save tracking or collect new answers. When you find prompts worth watching, add them in the app with **Track selected** on the Prompt Research page.

## When to use it

- Before choosing prompts to track.
- When you want prompt ideas for your market.
- To see which sites ChatGPT relies on for your topic.
- To compare interest across a few product categories or use cases.

For example: “What do people ask ChatGPT about invoicing for freelancers, and who gets cited?”

## What you get back

A short report with:

- the largest prompt groups and the strongest openings, where many questions come up and your brand rarely appears
- representative prompts for each group
- the domains cited most often in each group, and what kind of source they are, such as review articles, forums or competitor pages
- 10 to 20 exact prompts worth tracking

## How to read the results

The questions come from DataForSEO's dataset, built mostly from Google "People also ask" questions. They are not logged ChatGPT prompts, and no one can see how often a question is asked in AI, so the report shows no AI search volume. Prompt data covers US English only.

The prompts and citations come from DataForSEO's dataset, not from answers OpenSEO collected for you. To see dated answers from ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, track the prompts in Prompt Tracking, then use [AI Visibility Audit](/docs/skills/ai-visibility-audit) to act on them.

## How to get the best result

- Name the product categories or problems you want covered.
- Use short terms such as "crm" rather than long phrases. Long phrases return few prompts.
- Mention competitors you want to watch for in the citations.

Each research call uses usage credits. Results are cached for 24 hours. If you agree, the assistant can also ask ChatGPT the leading prompt directly to check today's answer and the searches behind it, which uses a small amount of credit.

## Use it with OpenSEO

Use the skill with SAM in the app, or connect an external assistant through [OpenSEO MCP](/docs/mcp) and [install the skills](/docs/skills/setup).
