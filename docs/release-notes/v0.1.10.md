Group similar keywords and search by city in Keyword Research. Also, a refreshed dark mode look + more.

## What's new

- Refreshed the app's look
  - Dark mode looks better
  - Pages load smoother
- City and county search volume in Keyword Research and MCP
- Keyword Research leaves out close variants unless "Group keywords" is on
- Pick the day and time scheduled rank checks run
- Let site audits through Shopify with a crawler access signature
  - Requires `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET` (32+ characters)

## Fixed

- Location search no longer cuts off same-named towns, like Springfield, Mo.
- Rank tracking charts and history update when a check finishes, without a reload.
- New site audits show up in the audit list without a reload.
- ChatGPT answers in Prompt Explorer no longer come back without sources.
