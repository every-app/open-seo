Google AI Mode in Prompt Tracking, movable local rank grids, and site audit path exclusions.

## What's new

- Track Google AI Mode in Prompt Tracking
- Move a local rank grid's center on a map
- Exclude URL paths from a site audit
- Edit Prompt Research keywords, in the app or with the new `update_ai_research_keywords` MCP tool
- More countries, including Somalia and Macao
- Self-host with a treg account instead of a DataForSEO account — thanks @JayZeeDesign
  - Set `TREG_API_TOKEN` in place of `DATAFORSEO_API_KEY`

## Fixed

- City and region search volume now works in Hong Kong, Taiwan, Israel and five other countries.
- Rank checks delayed by the search data provider keep waiting instead of failing, and the page says so.
- Connecting OpenSEO in ChatGPT or Claude again no longer signs out your earlier connection.
- The rank tracking table is now paged and no longer freezes on large trackers.
