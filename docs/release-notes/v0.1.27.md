Delete Local SEO businesses and change their area, Lighthouse results over MCP, and more complete site audits.

## What's new

- Delete a tracked business or change its area in Local SEO
- Read Lighthouse results with the new `get_audit_lighthouse` MCP tool
- Audits that stop at their page limit now say so
- `finalUrls` option on `get_serp_results` for page URLs instead of Google redirect links
  - Takes a few minutes instead of seconds

## Fixed

- Site audits that hit their page limit no longer use it up on tag pages and skip pages in your sitemap.
- Site audits no longer report a site's bot-check page as the real page.
- Keyword Research shows the keyword you searched in the overview card.
- `get_local_rank_grid` no longer counts a similarly named business as yours when you give its cid.
